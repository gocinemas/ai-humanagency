Questions people ask

Do you only work with big companies?

No. A lot of what we do suits small and medium businesses. We also work with teams inside large organisations.

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. We start with the tools you already pay for, and only build when buying won't do the job.

How does pricing work?

We agree the scope and the price up front for each piece of work, after a free first call.

Where are you based?

Surrey and London. We work remotely or on site, whichever suits the work.

What about our data?

We agree up front what data gets used, where it goes and who can see it. Where we can, we work inside your own accounts.

Do you build things, or just advise?

Both. We build our own AI products, so we can prototype and ship for you, not just write the plan.
