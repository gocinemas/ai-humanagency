03
Keep exploring

There is no wrong question. Here are four things to try in your first session:

  • Paste an email and ask it to write a clear, professional reply
  • Ask it to explain something you've always found confusing — in plain English
  • Paste a long document and ask for a 3-bullet summary
  • Ask "what are you actually good at?" and see what it says
