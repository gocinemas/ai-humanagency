Learning paths

Evals and harnesses, in order

Two short paths for anyone building with AI. Each one starts with the basics and ends with something you can do on Monday. Read them in order, or jump to the step you need.

Start here if you are new: an eval tells you whether the AI's output is good enough. A harness is everything around the model that makes it do real work reliably. Evals check the harness; the harness is what you improve.

Path 1: Evals (how to test AI output)

Work through these in order. Each step builds on the one before.

  1. 1Evals basicsWhy AI output needs testing, the two kinds of eval, and the error-analysis-first habit.
  2. 2Writing evals and rubricsHow to turn a failure you saw into a pass/fail judge, and how to check that judge against a human.
  3. 3Using tools to build evalsUsing AI assistants and eval platforms (Promptfoo, Braintrust, Langfuse) to speed up the work, without handing over the judgement.

How the two paths connect

Evals are how you know the harness works. Without evals, you are guessing. Without a harness, there is nothing consistent to evaluate. If you only have time for one path, do Evals step 1 and Harness step 1, then pick up the rest.
