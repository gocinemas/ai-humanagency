Learning paths

Evals and harnesses, in order

Two short paths for anyone building with AI. Each one starts with the basics and ends with something you can do on Monday. Read them in order, or jump to the step you need.

Start here: the two ideas in plain words

  1. →What is an eval?A repeatable test for AI answers: questions, a pass/fail rule, and a score you track over time.
  2. →What is a harness?Everything around the model that lets it do a real job: what it can see, what it can touch, and how you check it.

How the two paths connect

Evals are how you know the harness works. Without evals, you are guessing. Without a harness, there is nothing consistent to evaluate. If you only have time for one path, do Evals step 1 and Harness step 1, then pick up the rest.
