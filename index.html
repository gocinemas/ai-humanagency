Real Cost Economics

Vendor quotes are lies. Token pricing is not your actual cost. Model API (10-15%) is smaller than data engineering (25-40%), infrastructure (30-45%), and talent ($200k-$500k+).

Vendor Lock-In Traps

Small early decisions (embeddings format, prompt style, fine-tuning) become permanent. Switching vendors means re-embedding millions of vectors. Your system behavior depends on undocumented provider quirks.

You'll know: Which decisions lock you in (and how to avoid it). How to build a portable AI stack. What standardization actually looks like.
⏱️ 45 minutes

Production vs. Testing

Your demo works perfectly. Production fails across users, sessions, and days. 90% of "AI failures" disappear when integration tests are added. Why production is structurally different.

You'll know: What breaks between testing and production. How to architecture for safety. What testing won't catch (and why).
⏱️ 45 minutes

When to Use AI (& When Not To)

AI solves specific problems really well. It fails spectacularly at others. How to tell the difference before you've invested six months.

You'll know: Your realistic expectations. What "AI will do everything" costs (and why it fails). When a simple database query beats AI.
